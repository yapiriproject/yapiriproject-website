Why corpus quality matters

Natural sentences are the bridge to translation.

A large corpus becomes valuable only when the sentences are natural, translated accurately, consistently spelled, tagged, sourced, and reviewed. This is the foundation for educational translation, search, grammar checking, speech technology, and future machine translation.
