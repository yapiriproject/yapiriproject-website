Why the project exists

A language must live beyond memory.

Kokborok carries the history, identity, knowledge, and worldview of the Tiprasa people. Yet many words, expressions, stories, and traditional forms of knowledge remain undocumented, scattered, or difficult to access.

The Yapiri Project is building a permanent digital foundation so Kokborok can be studied, taught, written, translated, published, and used confidently in modern technology.

Community-centredEvidence-basedFuture-ready

Meet the writing system

Yapiri was created for Kokborok.

Yapiri is a writing system developed to represent Kokborok consistently while supporting education, publishing, digital communication, and future computing tools.

Purpose-builtDesigned specifically for Kokborok
UCSUR registeredDocumented within the Under-ConScript Unicode Registry
