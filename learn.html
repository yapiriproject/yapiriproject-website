Learn Kokborok through Yapiri.

A guided path from essential linguistic terms to letters, words, sentences, reading, writing, and translation practice.

Learning principle

Teach every script feature through meaningful Kokborok—not as isolated symbols disconnected from the language.

Guided path

A stable, progressive learning structure.

The lesson sequence below mirrors the learning architecture planned for the Yapiri ecosystem.

Introduction to Kokborok and Yapiri

Purpose, writing direction, Unicode status, and how the learning path works.

5 min

Terms to know

Vowels, consonants, diphthongs, nasals, stops, aspirates, glides, syllables, and tone.

12 min

Vowels and schwa

Core vowel forms, the Roman w correspondence, and word-based practice.

15 min

Consonants and digraphs

Stops, sonorants, aspirates, and examples such as khum, thaichuk, phantok, and chokhreng.

22 min

Syllables, tone, and punctuation

Build pronounceable units, distinguish tone, and write complete expressions.

18 min

Numerals 0–100

Recognise Yapiri numerals, type them, and reveal Kokborok number names.

20 min

Everyday pronouns and core words

Learn high-frequency vocabulary through examples and controlled recall.

30 min

Reading and translation practice

Move from word recognition to Roman–Yapiri reading and English-to-Kokborok translation.

Open practice
Practice hub

Recognition, recall, typing, and translation.

Each lesson should end with meaningful practice, immediate feedback, and a clear return path to the lesson rather than unexpected jumps.
