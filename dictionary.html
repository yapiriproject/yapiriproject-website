Why part of speech matters

Part of speech is not a decorative label. It controls how a word is defined, what grammatical information it needs, which examples are useful, and how dictionary entries connect to the grammar. Nouns may need number and case behaviour; verbs may need transitivity and aspect; pronouns need person and number; particles need their syntactic function.
